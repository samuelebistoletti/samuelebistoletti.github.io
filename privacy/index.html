Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Data controller

Samuele Bistoletti, reachable at samuele@bistoletti.me.

What data I process and why

  • Browsing. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These providers log technical data such as IP address, browser and requested page, for security and to run the service (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention follows their policies: GitHub, Cloudflare.
  • Statistics. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and measure page performance. It uses no cookies and, according to Cloudflare, does not collect visitors' personal data.
  • Email. If you write to me, I process what you send (name, email address, message) to reply and for any engagement that follows (pre-contractual and contractual steps, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). I keep it as long as needed for the request and any engagement, plus the periods required by law for tax records.
  • Booking a call. Bookings run on Cal.com. The data you enter (name, email, any notes) is processed by Cal.com under its privacy policy and shared with me to arrange the call (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Cookies

The site uses no profiling or analytics cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary technical cookies for security.

External links

Links to other sites (for example Octopus Energy, V2C, GitHub, LinkedIn) lead to services with their own privacy policies. Octopus Energy referral links and the V2C discount code are handled by those companies under their own terms.

Transfers outside the European Union

GitHub, Cloudflare and Cal.com may process data outside the EU, under the safeguards set by the GDPR such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing (Art. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to samuele@bistoletti.me. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
